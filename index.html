01 // About Me

Curiosity, Analytical Rigor & Craft

A student builder's journey driven by continuous experimentation, deep fundamentals, and deliberate practice.

I am a student fascinated by how simple mathematical rules and clean software architectures can capture complex dynamics — whether in financial markets or intuitive user interfaces.

My technical journey began with an innate curiosity about systems: understanding how data flows, how algorithms separate signal from market noise, and how clear digital interfaces communicate complex ideas without cognitive overhead.

In quantitative finance, I actively experiment on the WorldQuant BRAIN platform, researching mathematical alpha expressions, time-series decay, and portfolio turnover dynamics. I approach every simulation as a learning lab — testing hypotheses systematically and learning from drawdowns.

In web development, I believe in mastering fundamentals first: semantic HTML, modern CSS layouts, and clean code without unnecessary bloat. I strive to write code that is accessible, performant, and structured with intentionality.
